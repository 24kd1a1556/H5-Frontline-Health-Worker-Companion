# CHD — Childhood Illness: Diarrhoea (ORS + Zinc), Fever, Fast Breathing

> Simplified for the TalentAP hackathon from public Government of India guidance. Not for clinical use.
> Local terms: diarrhoea = *dast / loose motion / patle dast*; fever = *bukhar / taap*; cough = *khansi*; ORS = *jeevan rakshak ghol*; sunken eyes = *aankhein dhansi hui*.

## CHD-1 Diarrhoea (dast)

### CHD-1.1 Preparing ORS
- Mix **1 packet of ORS in 1 litre** of clean drinking water (boiled and cooled), in a clean container.
- **Throw away ORS solution after 24 hours** and make fresh.

### CHD-1.2 How much ORS to give
| Age | ORS after each loose stool |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 years | **50 to 100 ml** |
| 2 years or more | **100 to 200 ml** |

- Give frequent small sips from a cup (or spoon). If the child vomits, **wait 10 minutes**, then continue more slowly.
- Continue giving extra fluid until the diarrhoea stops. Give the mother **2 packets of ORS** to use at home.

### CHD-1.3 Zinc
| Age | Zinc dose (20 mg tablet) |
|---|---|
| 2 months up to 6 months | **half tablet (10 mg)** once a day |
| 6 months up to 5 years | **one full tablet (20 mg)** once a day |

- Give zinc for **14 days**, **even if the diarrhoea stops** earlier.
- Dissolve the tablet in a spoon of breast milk (for 2–6 months) or clean water (older children).
- Zinc is **not given to infants under 2 months**; a young infant with diarrhoea should be seen by the ANM/MO.

### CHD-1.4 Feeding during diarrhoea
**Continue feeding, including breastfeeding**, during diarrhoea, and give **extra feeds during and after the illness**. Do not stop food or breast milk.

### CHD-1.5 Checking for dehydration
| Classification | Signs |
|---|---|
| Severe dehydration | **two** of: lethargic or unconscious; sunken eyes; not able to drink or drinking poorly; skin pinch goes back **very slowly** (longer than 2 seconds) |
| Some dehydration | two of: restless, irritable; sunken eyes; drinks eagerly, thirsty; skin pinch goes back slowly |
| No dehydration | not enough signs for the above |

- **No dehydration:** ORS + zinc + feeding at home (CHD-1.2 to CHD-1.4).
- **Some dehydration:** the child needs ORS treatment under the supervision of the ANM/health facility (IMNCI Plan B); give ORS on the way.
- **Severe dehydration:** refer urgently (ESC-5.3).

### CHD-1.6 When to return or refer
Tell the mother to come back or go to the health facility **immediately** if the child becomes sicker, is not able to drink or breastfeed, is drinking poorly, has **blood in the stool**, or develops a fever. Blood in stool, diarrhoea for 14 days or more, or diarrhoea in a severely malnourished child → refer (ESC-5.3).

## CHD-2 Fever (bukhar) in children

### CHD-2.1 What counts as fever
Fever = the child feels hot (or has fever by history), or temperature **37.5 °C (99.5 °F) or above**. Always look for danger signs first (ESC-5.1, ESC-5.4); in a baby under 2 months see ESC-4.3.

### CHD-2.2 When a child with fever must be referred
- Fever with **any general danger sign** (not able to drink or breastfeed, vomits everything, convulsions, lethargic or unconscious) or a **stiff neck** → refer urgently (ESC-5.4).
- Fever present **every day for more than 7 days** → refer for assessment.

### CHD-2.3 Malaria and medicines
- In malaria-prone areas, a child with fever and no obvious other cause is tested for malaria (RDT) as per the national programme; treatment is given as per national guidelines by trained staff.
- The choice and dose of any medicine for fever or infection — **including paracetamol** and antibiotics — is decided by the ANM/Medical Officer; drug dosing is **out of scope** for this assistant (SCP-1.2).
- At home: keep giving fluids and breastfeeding, and come back if the child becomes sicker.

## CHD-3 Cough and fast breathing

### CHD-3.1 Counting breaths
Count the child's breaths for **one full minute** when the child is calm. Fast breathing is:

| Age | Fast breathing |
|---|---|
| Under 2 months | 60 breaths per minute or more |
| 2 months up to 12 months | **50 breaths per minute or more** |
| 12 months up to 5 years | **40 breaths per minute or more** |

A child with cough or difficult breathing **and** fast breathing, or with **chest indrawing** or **stridor** (a harsh noise when breathing in) when calm, must be referred (ESC-5.2; for babies under 2 months ESC-4.2).
