# NUT — Malnutrition Screening (MUAC) for Children

> Simplified for the TalentAP hackathon from public Government of India guidance. Not for clinical use.
> Local terms: malnutrition = *kuposhan*; very thin / wasted = *bahut dubla*; swelling of feet = *pair mein sujan*; MUAC tape = *baanh naapne ka feeta*.

## NUT-1 MUAC screening

### NUT-1.1 Who and how
- Screen **all children 6–59 months** with the MUAC tape (mid-upper arm circumference) and look for **bilateral pitting oedema** (oedema of both feet).
- MUAC is used only for children **6 months or older**. Infants under 6 months are not measured with MUAC (see NUT-2.4).
- Oedema check: press gently on the top of both feet; if a dent (pit) remains in **both** feet, the child has bilateral pitting oedema.
- In Chandrapuri Block, MUAC is re-measured every month by the ASHA/AWW (block arrangement; VIS-5.2).

### NUT-1.2 MUAC classification (children 6–59 months)
| Result | MUAC | Colour on the tape |
|---|---|---|
| Severe acute malnutrition (SAM) | **below 11.5 cm**, or **oedema of both feet** at any MUAC | red |
| Moderate acute malnutrition (MAM) | **11.5–12.4 cm** | yellow |
| No acute malnutrition | **12.5 cm or more** | green |

SAM is also diagnosed by the ANM/MO when weight-for-height is below −3 SD.

## NUT-2 What to do

### NUT-2.1 SAM with complications → NRC immediately
A SAM child with **any** complication — poor appetite (fails the appetite test), visible severe wasting, **oedema of both feet**, severe palmar pallor, lethargy/drowsiness/unconsciousness, continual irritability, any breathing difficulty, or signs of severe dehydration — must be referred **immediately** to the Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre (NRC). See ESC-5.5.

### NUT-2.2 SAM without complications
A SAM child (MUAC below 11.5 cm) who is alert, has no oedema and no other complication must be taken to the ANM/CHO/Medical Officer for a **medical check and appetite test**. Children who **fail the appetite test** are referred to the NRC; children who pass are managed in the community under the CMAM protocol, with regular follow-up by the AWW/ASHA.

### NUT-2.3 MAM
For a child with MAM (MUAC 11.5–12.4 cm): counsel the family on feeding (more frequent, energy-dense meals and continued breastfeeding), make sure the child gets supplementary nutrition from the Anganwadi centre, check for illness and immunisation, and re-measure MUAC every month.

### NUT-2.4 Infants under 6 months
Do not use MUAC. Any infant under 6 months who is **visibly wasted**, has **oedema**, or is **too weak or feeble to suckle** must be referred immediately (ESC-5.5). Support exclusive breastfeeding.

### NUT-2.5 Feeding advice to prevent malnutrition
Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months; start complementary food at 6 months while continuing breastfeeding; feed extra during and after illness.
